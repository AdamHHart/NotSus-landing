NotSus for Android tablets

This is the early version. It works, and families are using it. We are still setting up on the Google Play Store, so for now you install it straight from us instead of from Play.

That means Android will show you some warnings during the install. They are normal for any app that does not come from the Play Store yet. The steps below walk you through each one, in the order you will see them.

Installing it, step by step

Do this on the tablet itself, not on a computer. The whole thing takes about two minutes.

  1. Tap the download button above. Your browser will ask whether you are sure, because the file is an app rather than a picture or a document: "This type of file can harm your device. Do you want to keep NotSus-2.2.0.apk anyway?" Choose Download anyway, or OK.
  2. Open the file when it finishes. Tap the download notification, or find it in your Files app under Downloads.
  3. Allow this one install. The first time, Android will say something close to: "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source." Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then press back. On Samsung tablets the switch is called Install unknown apps. This permission applies only to the browser you downloaded with, and you can turn it off again afterwards.
  4. Tell Play Protect to go ahead. Google checks apps that did not come from the Play Store: "Unsafe app blocked" or "App scan recommended" Tap More details, then Install anyway. Play Protect says this about every app it has not seen on the Play Store before, including ours.
  5. Tap Install, then Open. NotSus will ask you to make a family account, or sign in to the one you already have.

What to expect from the early version

Updates come from here, not automatically. When there is a new version we will email you and you download it from this page again. Installing over the top keeps your account, your children's profiles and your settings. Once NotSus is on the Play Store, updates arrive on their own.

Everything is the finished app. The filtering, the daily time limits, the site requests and the parent page all work the same as they will at launch. Early access describes how you install it, and nothing about what it does.

If something goes wrong, tell us. Write to contact@notsus.net and say what tablet you have and what happened. We read every one.

Questions parents ask

Is it safe to install an app this way? The warnings are Android being careful, which is a good thing. They appear because the app is not on the Play Store yet, and not because anything is wrong with it. The file is signed by NotSus Education Inc., which is how your tablet can tell a real NotSus update from anything pretending to be one.

Does my child need a Google account? No. NotSus uses the family account you make with us.
