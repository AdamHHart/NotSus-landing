Delete your NotSus account

NotSus: Browser for Kids, made by NotSus Education Inc.

You can delete your family account at any time. It takes your account, your children's accounts, and everything saved in them. It cannot be undone.

In the app

  1. Open NotSus and sign in as the parent.
  2. Open the profile menu, then choose Settings.
  3. On your own tab, choose Delete my account.
  4. Confirm, and enter your parent PIN.

If you no longer have the app

Write to contact@notsus.net from the email address on the account, and ask us to delete it. We will confirm when it is done.

What is deleted, and what is kept

When you delete your account, we delete it, your children's accounts, and the information in them, straight away. That includes:

Deleted straight away Your name and email. Each child's profile, display name and PIN. The sites you approved. Your children's goals, projects and notes. Activity, time used, and site requests.
Deleted on a schedule, whether or not you delete your account Blocked sites, including anything typed into a search box: 90 days. Crash reports: 90 days. The record of which project pages were opened: 30 days.
Kept for a short time after Ordinary server logs may hold a record of the request. They are not linked to your account once it is gone.
Not ours to delete Payment records held by Apple, Google or Stripe, which they keep under their own rules and tax law.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription bought through Apple or Google. Those belong to your store account, and only you can cancel them there: on an iPad, Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android, Google Play, your profile, Payments and subscriptions.

A subscription bought on notsus.net is cancelled when the account is deleted.

You can also read our privacy policy and terms.
